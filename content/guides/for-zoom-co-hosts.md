# For Zoom Co-Hosts

- Be warned:   
  ![Zoom's "End meeting for all" button above "Leave meeting"](images/guide-for-zoom-co-hosts-1.png)  
  Be careful not to accidentally click the “End meeting for all” button when you leave the room\! Everyone would need to rejoin.  
  - Be really careful. This button has been misclicked by trained persons many times in past major events.   
  - Just imagine. This button appears alongside “Leave Meeting” when you try to close the Zoom window. When you need to leave a meeting in a hurry, you don’t like being offered a choice, and you tend to dismiss that choice.  
- If you kick a person from Zoom, that kicked Zoom account won’t be able to join again.

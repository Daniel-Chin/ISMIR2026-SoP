# Promote to & Demote from Panelist

In a Zoom Webinar, attendees can only watch. To let someone speak or share their screen, a host or co-host promotes them to panelist.

- First, click **Participants** in the Zoom toolbar.  
  ![The "Participants" button in the Zoom toolbar](images/participants_button.png)
- Then, in the **Attendees** tab, hover over the person's name, click **More**, then **Promote to Panelist**.  
  ![The "Promote to Panelist" item for an attendee](images/promote_to_panelist.png)
- The person briefly leaves and rejoins the webinar as a panelist. Tell them this may take a few seconds. Once promoted, they can unmute, start video and share screen.
- You can also demote a panelist back to attendee: in the **Panelists** tab, hover over the name, click **More**, then **Change Role to Attendee**.  
  ![The "Change Role to Attendee" item for a panelist](images/demote_to_attendee.png)
- Zoom display names are not always real names. Use [#panelist-names](https://ismir2026.slack.com/archives/C0CER5XPX19) to identify users.

# For All Volunteers

- Change your displayed name on Slack to “Name (Volunteer)”.  
  - (People will be more likely to answer your research questions\!)  
  - Additional steps for internal management:  
    Open your own profile.  
    ![Click Edit next to "About me" in your Slack profile](images/guide-for-all-volunteers-1.png)  
    ![Under Tags, pick "On-site volunteer" or "Online volunteer"](images/guide-for-all-volunteers-2.png)  
- When taking a job in [\#help](https://ismir2026.slack.com/archives/C0C45QQMU6A), respond to the request (e.g. “Let me help you with this”) in \#help so others know you are already on it.

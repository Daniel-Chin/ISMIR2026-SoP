# Assisting with Zoom

- When sharing screen, there’s a tickbox “Share Sound”. If not checked, laptop sounds will not be shared to Zoom.  
  ![The "Share" button in the Zoom toolbar](images/share-screen_button.png) ![The "Share sound" tickbox under "Share Options"](images/share-sound_button.png)  
- Zoom supports “Original Sound for Musicians” that does not remove music as noise. It’s in audio settings.  
  ![The "Original sound for musicians" item in the Audio dropdown menu](images/original_sound_for_musicians.png)  
- To avoid echo feedback in special situations, a useful trick is to “Leave Computer Audio” from the Audio dropdown menu. This mutes the mic & mutes the speaker.  
  ![The "Leave computer audio" item in the Audio dropdown menu](images/leave_computer_audio.png)

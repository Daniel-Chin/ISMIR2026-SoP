# Assisting with Slack

- We created a channel for each paper and each event. Press Ctrl+K  to search. Or, click "Browse channels" on the left pane:  
  ![The ⋮ menu next to "Channels" in the left pane](images/guide-assisting-with-slack-1.png) ![The "Browse channels" menu item](images/guide-assisting-with-slack-2.png)  
- Attendees should remember to right click and Star their channels of interest.  
- Directory of some channels:  
  - [\#general](https://ismir2026.slack.com/archives/C0C498S6X5X). The virtual spotlight. Find announcements here. 🎤  
  - [\#help](https://ismir2026.slack.com/archives/C0C45QQMU6A). Ask questions here to IT, Chairs, and volunteers. 🙋‍♀️  
  - [\#social](https://ismir2026.slack.com/archives/C0C4140HN3D). One place for old and new friends\! 🎈  
  - [\#random](https://ismir2026.slack.com/archives/C0C4B0AK092). Anything goes.  
- Reading pinned messages in [\#general](https://ismir2026.slack.com/archives/C0C498S6X5X) can help you a long way. Simply press the “Pins” tab near the top when you are in the channel.  
- Slack can be unintuitive how it hides a lot of contents from the left pane by default.  
  - Channels that you have not joined, but have access to, are hidden.  
  - Even Direct-Message (DM) conversations, if deemed inactive, are hidden.  
  - The only way that is sure to find anything, is to click the search bar or press Ctrl+K, and search with keywords.

# Hosting a Hybrid Discussion

You host one room of a hybrid discussion: an on-site room joined to a Zoom breakout room through the room computer (camera + mic + speakers). Your job is to make sure the online participants are equal members of the conversation.

## Hybrid Q&A rules

- **Every on-site voice goes through a mic.** If it isn't in the mic, online participants can't hear it. If someone starts speaking without one, pause them politely until the mic arrives.
- **Repeat or summarize** when something was said off-mic anyway.
- **Check online regularly.** Every few minutes, turn to the screen and ask the online participants whether they want to add something. Online hands are easy to miss: keep an eye on raised hands and the Zoom chat.
- **Alternate** between on-site and online speakers when both are waiting.
- **Read out the chat** when online participants type instead of speaking.
- **Don't let the mic go back too fast.** Let the [[Mic Runner]] keep the mic with a speaker for a multi-round exchange.
- **Echo**: if you hear echo or feedback, the room computer is the only device in the room allowed to have Zoom audio on. Ask on-site participants who joined Zoom on their laptop to "Leave Computer Audio".

## When something breaks

- Room system (camera, mic, speakers, screen): in A6-007/A6-008 call the [[Venue AV/IT]]; in A6-004/A6-005 call the [[Zoom Master]].
- Zoom breakout room issues: message the [[Zoom Master]].
- Keep the discussion going on-site while it is fixed; type a short note in the Zoom chat so online participants know.

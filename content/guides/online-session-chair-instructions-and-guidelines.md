# Online Session Chair: Instructions and Guidelines

Thank you for chairing a paper session at ISMIR 2026! The role of the session chairs is to manage a particular paper session by introducing the authors, ensuring the session runs on time, and making the session more interesting for both in-person and remote attendees. Each paper session has one [[On-site Session Chair]] and one [[Online Session Chair]] (you), supported by volunteers on-site and online. You are the host for the online attendees.

## Your paper session at a glance

- A paper session lasts 90 minutes, with 10 papers presented in it. Each paper session has an on-site and an online session chair. 
- **Oral part (30 min):** 10 back-to-back 3-minute presentations, no Q&A. On-site presenters present on stage;
for online presenters, the pre-recorded video is played. Streamed over Zoom Webinar.
- **Poster part (60 min, with coffee):** every paper has a poster with a TV and a laptop in a Zoom breakout room, 
so online attendees and online presenters can join. 
- Papers are assigned to sessions by author timezone and mode of presentation (online/in-person), so your session 
will be a mix of on-site and online presenters.
- You will be supported by an online session volunteer.

## Before the conference

- Join the Slack channels of your session: #oral-session-?, #poster-session-?, and #oral-session-?-presenters.
- Say hello to your [[On-site Session Chair]]. Agree on who contacts which presenters, and how you'll reach each 
other during the session (e.g. a Slack DM).
- Optional but encouraged: pre-read the 10 papers. Prepare one question per paper, to start discussions in quiet breakout rooms. Prepare any trivia or one-liners or take home messages from the papers that you could post during the session to gather interest in the papers
- Get to know the presenters on Slack, especially the online ones:
  - Confirm their pre-recorded video is on Miniconf.
  - Confirm they will join their Zoom breakout room during the Poster part, and that they use the [Zoom App](https://zoom.us/download), not the web client.
  - Tell the [[On-site Session Chair]] about any change.
- Test your own setup: Zoom App, Slack, and a stable connection.

## On the day

Follow your runsheets (linked above) for the minute-by-minute steps. Suggestions for the hybrid parts:

### Oral part (first 30 min)

- Join the Zoom Webinar 10 minutes early, with #oral-session-? open on Slack.
- Introduce yourself and the on-site session chair on Slack and welcome attendees to the session online. 
- At the start, welcome the online attendees in #oral-session-? and post the presentation order with Miniconf links.
- As each talk starts, start a Slack thread for it, so online attendees can post questions. There is no Q&A in the Oral part; the questions are taken to the Poster part. Please feel free to post any trivia or take home messages to engage with the online attendees. 
- If the stream has problems, report in [#vol-alerts](https://ismir2026.slack.com/archives/C0CDNPPNJ0Z). The [[Webinar Sentinel Online]] is also watching.
- At the end, direct online attendees to #poster-session-? for the Poster Session Zoom link.

### Poster part (60 min, with coffee)

- In the first 10 minutes, check that each online presenter of your session is in their breakout room. If one is missing, ping them on Slack and tell the [[Poster Session Helpdesk Online]].
- Hop between the breakout rooms of your session, starting with the quiet ones. Use your prepared questions to start discussions.
- Relay the questions from the Slack threads to the presenters, and tag the presenters in the threads.
- If online visitors are waiting at a poster with an on-site presenter, tell the [[On-site Session Chair]] so they can let the presenter know.
- At the end, thank the presenters in #poster-session-?.

An oral session is a sequence of short talks, each followed by Q&A. The audience is both in the room and on Zoom, and both should feel equally able to ask questions.

## Principles

- **Time is sacred.** The [[Session Chair]] keeps every talk to its slot, so later speakers are not squeezed.
- **Remote is first-class.** The [[Zoom Volunteer]] makes sure remote questions get asked, not just room ones.
- **The room just works.** The [[Room Volunteer]]s handle microphones and time cards, so the chair can focus on the speakers.

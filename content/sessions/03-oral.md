- Runs 30 minutes. 
- Onsite location: 300-capacity auditorium A6-008.
- 10 presentations, each 3 minutes. Back to back, no Q&A.
- If the presenter is on-site, present on stage.
- Otherwise, play the pre-recorded video already on Miniconf.
- The [[On-site Session Chair]] introduces every talk. Check name pronunciations beforehand. Be brief, nice, and non-distracting.
- On-site presenters sit in the front row. Presentation uses the stage laptop by default.
- Streamed over Zoom Webinar. 
  - A [[Webinar Sentinel]] sits on site but views only the stream.
  - [[On-site Session Chair]] mic & presenter mic route through PA system into Zoom sound input. Laptop audio runs through Zoom Webinar Screenshare.

### Stage setup
- One hosting laptop, preferably MacBook (for maximum compatibility). 
  - Keep open the [Sheet for Session Chair](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JPkY1TY8BfC07ZicIe6FDiZt94PALu3r6AFXhavpwlY/edit?gid=1217880391#gid=1217880391). 
  - One window at a time. Open slides, close slides. Open video, close video.
  - Issue: One laptop = single point of failure?
    - Hard drive / thumb drive

### Slides collection
- The on-site presenters submit their slides directly to the Virtual Team. The Virtual Team prepares the [Sheet for Session Chair](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JPkY1TY8BfC07ZicIe6FDiZt94PALu3r6AFXhavpwlY/edit?gid=1217880391#gid=1217880391). The Virtual Team is responsible for reminding authors about overdue slides.
- Deadline: 2 days before the conference.
- Slides format:
  - A PDF file is required for failsafe. 
  - The presenter may additionally upload a Powerpoint file, a Keynote file, or a link to Google Slides (make sure to set the sharing settings to "Anyone with the link can view").
  - File size: <= 50 MB each.
- On the conference day, when authors request to update slides, the [[On-site Session Chair]] directly overwrites the URL in the "slides_*" column of Google Sheet without needing help from the Virtual Team.

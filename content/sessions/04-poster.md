- Runs 60 minutes.
- Hybrid. By each poster is a TV that serves as a window to the online participants.
- One Zoom breakout room per paper. Not streamed on YouTube or Webinar.
- Onsite location: Upper Atrium, A6. 
- Each paper has: a printed landscape/portrait A0 poster + a laptop (on podium) + a TV + 2 cabled handheld directional mics + 1 simple audio interface.
- Each laptop enters a Zoom breakout room. The audio input is 2 cabled handheld mics via the audio interface. The video input is the laptop webcam. If the presenter is remote, they screenshare the poster. 
- Train on-site presenters to consciously divert attention to online participants.
- If the presenter is on-site: 
  - On-site discussions are encouraged to use the mics for the online to hear.
  - Question: will the inconvenience discourage discussions?
  - Online participants use Zoom. For questions, they are instructed to raise their hands or open mic. 
  - Face the laptop webcam towards the poster and the author for handwavy pointing.
- If the presenter is online:
  - Better with big TV for the online presenter to share screen and move cursor. 
  - The handheld mic is free to take and pass around.
  - Face the laptop webcam towards the visitors.
- Online participants are directed to the Slack channel “Poster Session x”, which has the link to the Poster Session Zoom room containing a breakout room for each paper. For online participants to view the poster, use the miniconf paper page, entering from either the Live Overview, the miniconf paper list, or the Slack channel.
- Live Overview shows realtime online headcount at each poster.

### Troubleshoot cheatsheet
- Zoom’s web-browser client has limited features. Instruct participants to download and install the [Zoom App](https://zoom.us/download).
- “Original sound for musicians” can be turned on to stop the noise filter from dropping sounds.
- Crosstalk between posters can be mitigated by lowering mic gain on the audio interface.

### Day-start room preparation
- Set up 10 poster kiosks. Each laptop: has Zoom client, log into a unique Zoom account, keep a URL to / window of miniconf, and log into Slack using a non-Admin account.
- Set up a material desk. Provide pushpins, tapes, and markers.
- Set up a helpdesk with a checked-out laptop. Install Zoom client, log into the Zoom Meeting host account, and keep a URL to / window of miniconf.

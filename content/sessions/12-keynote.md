- Runs 60 minutes.
- Onsite location: 300-capacity auditorium A6-008.
- Streamed via Zoom Webinar 500. 

### Q&A
- On-site & online questions are supported. We support not only a Q&A session but also live speaker-audience interaction.
- During the Q&A, take on-site questions first, then online questions. No need to switch back and forth.
- Online questions are text only, via the Zoom Webinar Q&A tool. Raising hands is not supported.
- For text questions in the Q&A tool, a [[Q&A Producer]] organizes and clarifies the questions before relaying them. 
- Use cards / gestures for robust & confident flow. When there is a clarified question, no matter during the talk or during Q&A, the [[Q&A Producer]] signals (e.g. raises a hand). When the Speaker takes the question, the [[Q&A Producer]] reads it.
  - If the Speaker is remote, the [[Q&A Producer]] has to barge in some of the times, "We have a question. Is now a good time?".
- When the Speaker takes an on-site question, a [[Mic Runner]] gives their mic to the person asking.

### Stage setup
- Two stage laptops, preferably MacBook (for maximum compatibility). 

### Tech things
- The Speaker may use any laptop for slides. That laptop is connected to Auditorium HDMI.
- Audio mix that goes into the Zoom Webinar sound input:
  - Speaker mic
  - [[Keynote Inviter]] mic
  - [[Q&A Producer]] mic
  - [[Mic Runner]]s mic
- On-site slides audio goes through Auditorium system into Webinar. Online slides audio goes via screenshare.

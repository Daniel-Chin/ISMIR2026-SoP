- No predefined program or invited speakers. Participants propose topics on [Dotstorming](https://dotstorming.com/b/6aea16167c45b114dc388cf8) before the session; anyone can propose, comment, or express interest.
- Topics: anything MIR-relevant (technical challenges, research directions, community initiatives, social/ethical issues, ...).
- Formats: discussions, short informal presentations, demos, brainstorming, etc. No preparation required.

### Flow
1. **Short plenary**: introduce the proposed topics and identify those attracting the most interest.
2. **Breakout**: participants split into smaller groups, on-site or online, for ~45 minutes of informal discussion.

### Rooms
- 4 hybrid rooms. Each on-site room is paired with one Zoom breakout room; the on-site room joins its breakout room as one Zoom account (room computer + camera + mic).
- Each room has an [[Unconference Room Host]] who facilitates and balances on-site and online participation.
- One of the 4 hosts is the [[Unconference Chair]], who also runs the opening plenary and makes a concluding statement to everyone, on-site and online.
- Breakout rooms are a Zoom *Meeting* feature (not Webinar), so this session runs on a Zoom Meeting.

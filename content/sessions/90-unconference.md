- No predefined program or invited speakers. Participants propose topics on [Dotstorming](https://dotstorming.com/b/6aea16167c45b114dc388cf8) before the session; anyone can propose, comment, or express interest.
- Topics: anything MIR-relevant (technical challenges, research directions, community initiatives, social/ethical issues, ...).
- Formats: discussions, short informal presentations, demos, brainstorming, etc. No preparation required.

### Flow
1. **Opening plenary** (hybrid) in A6-008, on the Zoom Meeting hub. Participants vote on topics online using Dotstorming; the top 4 topics are taken.
2. **Online breakouts open** right after the online vote: all 4 topics, as 4 Zoom breakout rooms. Online participants do not wait for the on-site vote.
3. **On-site vote**: on-site participants vote among the top 4 to select 2 topics. The [[Unconference Chair]] announces each on-site topic with its room (A6-008 or A6-007).
4. **Discussion** (~45 min), on-site and online groups run separately (not hybrid):
   - **On-site**: the 2 on-site topics go to A6-008 and A6-007. A6-008 disconnects from Zoom.
   - **Online**: the 4 topics in 4 Zoom breakout rooms.
5. **Reconvene** (hybrid): online breakout rooms close; on-site participants walk back to A6-008, which reconnects to the Zoom hub. Each group's [[Unconference Room Host]] shares its conclusions: 4 online and 2 on-site.
6. The Chair closes the session.

### Rooms
- 6 groups: 2 on-site (A6-008, A6-007) and 4 online (Zoom breakout rooms). Neither on-site room is connected to Zoom during discussion.
- [[Venue AV/IT]] supports A6-008's room system, including disconnecting and reconnecting it to Zoom.
- Each group has:
  - 1 volunteer ([[Unconference Room Volunteer On-Site]] or [[Unconference Room Volunteer Online]]), pre-assigned. Makes sure people can join, makes sure someone takes the Room Host role, and keeps time.
  - 1 [[Unconference Room Host]], not necessarily pre-assigned: can emerge during the discussion. Moderates, keeps notes, and presents conclusions at the reconvene.
- The Unconference Chair and the other organizer (Preeti) do not take breakout room roles.
- Anyone can join any breakout room.
- Online participants see a notice in the hub telling them how to choose a breakout room.
- Breakout rooms are a Zoom *Meeting* feature (not Webinar), so this session runs on a Zoom Meeting.

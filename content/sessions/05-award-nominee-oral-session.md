- Oral presentations of the award-nominated papers. 
- Each paper gets 10-min presentation and 5-min Q&A. 
- Some presenters are on-site; some are remote and present live via Zoom Webinar. 
- Onsite location: 300-capacity auditorium A6-008. (But A6-007 on Wednesday afternoon.)
- Streamed via Zoom Webinar 500.

### Presenter Preparation
- Use a separate Zoom Meeting as the prep room for online joiners.
- An online presenter joins the prep room 5 minutes before the scheduled presentation.
- A [[Prep Room Assistant]] helps the online presenter figure out audio+video input+output, as well as screenshare with sound. 
- When it’s time, let the presenter quit the prep room and join the main Webinar. 
- to json: The [[Zoom Master]] manually promotes the presenter to panelist.
- to json: exchange zoom display name

### Q&A
- Each presentation is followed by its Q&A. Questions do not interrupt the presentation.
- On-site & online questions are supported.
- Two channels exist for online questions:
  - Zoom Webinar Q&A tool.
  - Zoom Webinar raise hand & unmute. (No video)
- For text questions in the Q&A tool, a [[Q&A Tracker]] organizes the questions before relaying them. Text questions arriving during the presentation will wait for the Q&A.
- The [[Award Session Chair]] starts and ends each Q&A, and picks the questions, balancing online VS on-site questions.
- Use cards / gestures for robust & confident flow. When there is a clarified online question, the [[Q&A Tracker]] signals the [[Award Session Chair]] (e.g. raises a hand). When the [[Award Session Chair]] takes the question, the [[Q&A Tracker]] reads it / unmutes the attendee and calls him/her to speak up.
- When the [[Award Session Chair]] takes an on-site question, a [[Mic Runner]] gives their mic to the person asking.

### Slides collection
- These 10-minute slides are distinct from the 3-min slides for [Oral Session](../oral-session/).
- The on-site presenters submit their slides directly to the Virtual Team. The Virtual Team prepares the [Sheet for Award Session Chair](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JPkY1TY8BfC07ZicIe6FDiZt94PALu3r6AFXhavpwlY/edit?gid=332004949#gid=332004949). The Virtual Team is responsible for reminding authors about overdue slides.
- Deadline: 2 days before the conference.
- Slides format:
  - A PDF file is required for failsafe. 
  - The presenter may additionally upload a Powerpoint file, a Keynote file, or a link to Google Slides (make sure to set the sharing settings to "Anyone with the link can view").
  - File size: <= 50 MB each.
- After the deadline, when authors request to update slides, the [[Award Session Chair]] may manually overwrite the URL in the "slides_*" column of Google Sheet without needing help from the Virtual Team.
- The online presenters manage their own slides.

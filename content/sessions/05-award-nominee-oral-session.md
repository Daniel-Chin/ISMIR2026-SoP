Oral presentations of the award-nominated papers. Speakers present one after another. Some Speakers are on-site; some are remote and present live via Zoom. Each talk is followed by its own Q&A.

The technical setup is the same as [Keynote](../keynote/). Q&A is similar to Keynote, except that questions are taken only in the separate Q&A after each talk.

- Onsite location: 300-capacity auditorium A6-008.
- Streamed via Zoom Webinar 500.
- Zoom Chat is disabled for attendees.

### Q&A
- Each talk is followed by its own Q&A. Questions do not interrupt the talk.
- On-site & online questions are supported.
- Two channels exist for online questions:
  - Zoom Webinar Q&A tool.
  - Zoom Webinar raise hand & unmute. (No video)
- For text questions in the Q&A tool, a [[Q&A Producer]] organizes and clarifies the questions before relaying them. Text questions arriving during the talk will wait for the Q&A.
- The [[On-site Session Chair]] starts and ends each Q&A, and picks the questions, balancing online VS on-site questions.
- Use cards / gestures for robust & confident flow. When there is a clarified online question, the [[Q&A Producer]] signals the [[On-site Session Chair]] (e.g. raises a hand). When the [[On-site Session Chair]] takes the question, the [[Q&A Producer]] reads it / unmutes the attendee and calls him/her to speak up.
  - If the Speaker is remote, the [[Q&A Producer]] also says it out loud, e.g. "We have an online question."
- For on-site questions, a [[Mic Runner]] gives their mic to the person asking.

### Speakers and switching
- All Speakers, on-site and remote, are set up before the session starts, and then stay in the Zoom Webinar.
  - On-site Speakers wait in the front row. Their laptop stays in the Zoom Webinar, with audio+video disconnected.
  - Remote Speakers wait in the Zoom Webinar, muted and with camera off.
- During each talk, the next Speaker's readiness is confirmed again:
  - On-site: the [[On-site Session Chair]] confirms the Speaker is in the front row, with the laptop still in the Zoom Webinar.
  - Remote: the [[Zoom Master]] confirms the Speaker is still in the Zoom Webinar.
- Switching to the next Speaker, after the Q&A:
  - The finished Speaker stops screenshare. If on-site, they take their laptop back to the front row.
  - The [[On-site Session Chair]] introduces the next Speaker.
  - If the next Speaker is on-site, they bring their laptop to the stage and start screenshare.
  - If the next Speaker is remote, they unmute, start video, and start screenshare.
- If the next Speaker is not ready on their turn, move on to the following talk. The skipped talk is given later; the [[On-site Session Chair]] decides when.
  - For a remote Speaker, the [[Zoom Master]] walks to the side of the stage to tell the [[On-site Session Chair]].

### Time cues
- On-site Speakers: the [[On-site Session Chair]] reminds them of time elapsed.
- Remote Speakers: the [[Zoom Master]] reminds them of time elapsed via Zoom private chat.
- During Q&A, the [[On-site Session Chair]] keeps track of time.

### Tech things
- Each on-site Speaker uses their own laptop for slides. That slides laptop is connected via Zoom Webinar, and not Auditorium HDMI.
- Audio mix that goes into the Zoom Webinar sound input:
  - Speaker mic (fixed at the podium, no handover between Speakers)
  - [[On-site Session Chair]] mic
  - [[Q&A Producer]] mic
  - [[Mic Runner]]s mic
- Slides audio goes through Zoom screenshare.

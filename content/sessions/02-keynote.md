- Runs 60 minutes.
- Onsite location: 300-capacity auditorium A6-008.
- Streamed via Zoom Webinar 500. 

### Q&A
- On-site & online questions are supported. We support not only a Q&A session but also live speaker-audience interaction.
- Two channels exist for online questions: 
  - Zoom Webinar Q&A tool. 
  - Zoom Webinar raise hand & unmute. (No video)
- For text questions in the Q&A tool, a [[Q&A Producer]] organizes and clarifies the questions before relaying them. 
- Use cards / gestures for robust & confident flow. When there is a clarified question, no matter during the talk or during Q&A, the [[Q&A Producer]] signals (e.g. raises a hand). When the Speaker takes the question, the [[Q&A Producer]] reads it / unmutes the attendee and calls him/her to speak up.
  - If the Speaker is remote, the [[Q&A Producer]] has to barge in some of the times, "We have a question. Is now a good time?".
- When the [[On-site Session Chair]] takes an on-site question, a [[Mic Runner]] gives their mic to the person asking.

### Stage setup
- One stage laptop, preferably MacBook (for maximum compatibility). 
  - Keep open the [Sheet for Session Chair](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JPkY1TY8BfC07ZicIe6FDiZt94PALu3r6AFXhavpwlY/edit?gid=1217880391#gid=1217880391). 
  - Keep open the local folder containing all downloaded files.
  - One window at a time. Open slides, close slides. Open video, close video.
  - Issue: One laptop = single point of failure?

### Slides collection
- The on-site presenters submit their slides directly to the Virtual Team. The Virtual Team prepares the [Sheet for Session Chair](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JPkY1TY8BfC07ZicIe6FDiZt94PALu3r6AFXhavpwlY/edit?gid=1217880391#gid=1217880391). The Virtual Team is responsible for reminding authors about overdue slides.
- Deadline: 2 days before the conference.
- Slides format:
  - A PDF file is required for failsafe. 
  - The presenter may additionally upload a Powerpoint file, a Keynote file, or a link to Google Slides (make sure to set the sharing settings to "Anyone with the link can view").
  - File size: <= 50 MB each.
- After the deadline, when authors request to update slides, the [[On-site Session Chair]] may manually overwrite the URL in the "slides_*" column of Google Sheet without needing help from the Virtual Team.

### Tech things

- Audio mix that goes into the Zoom Webinar sound input:
  - Speaker mic
  - [[Keynote Inviter]] mic
  - [[Q&A Producer]] mic
  - [[Mic Runner]]s mic
- On-site slides audio goes through Auditorium system into Webinar. Online slides audio goes via screenshare.

- Talks followed by Q&A, like other talk+Q&A sessions.
- Hybrid, using Zoom Webinar. **All speakers are online** (Zoom panelists); slides are screenshared from their own computers.

### Q&A
- On-site & online questions are supported.
- During Q&A: on-site questions first, then online questions. No switching back and forth.
- Online questions are text only, via the Zoom Webinar Q&A tool. Raising hands is not supported.
- The [[Q&A Tracker]] sits on stage, relays online questions to the Speaker, and marks each as answered.
- Because the Speaker is remote, the [[Q&A Tracker]] may need to barge in: "We have a question. Is now a good time?".
- When the Speaker takes an on-site question, a [[Mic Runner]] gives their mic to the person asking.

### Tech things
- Audio mix that goes into the Zoom Webinar sound input:
  - [[Q&A Tracker]] mic
  - [[Mic Runner]]s mic
- Online speakers' audio (voice + slides sound share) comes via Zoom and plays through the Auditorium system.

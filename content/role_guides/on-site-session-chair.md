# On-site Session Chair: Instructions and Guidelines

Thank you for chairing a paper session at ISMIR 2026! The role of the session chairs is to manage a particular paper 
session by introducing the authors, ensuring the session runs on time, and making the session more interesting for 
both in-person and remote attendees. Each paper session has one [[On-site Session Chair]] (you) and 
one [[Online Session Chair]], supported by volunteers on-site and online.

## Your paper session at a glance

- A paper session lasts 90 minutes, with 10 papers presented in it. Each paper session has an on-site and an online session chair. 
- **Oral part (30 min):** 10 back-to-back 3-minute presentations, no Q&A. On-site presenters present on stage;
for online presenters, the pre-recorded video is played. Streamed over Zoom Webinar.
- **Poster part (60 min, with coffee):** every paper has a poster with a TV and a laptop in a Zoom breakout room, 
so online attendees and online presenters can join. 
- Papers are assigned to sessions by author timezone and mode of presentation (online/in-person), so your session 
will be a mix of on-site and online presenters.
- You will be supported by an on-site session volunteer.

## Before the conference

- Join the Slack channels of your session: #oral-session-?, #poster-session-?, and most importantly #oral-session-?-presenters.
- Say hello to your [[Online Session Chair]]. Agree on who contacts which presenters, and how you'll reach each other during the session (e.g. a Slack DM).
- Optional but encouraged: pre-read the 10 papers. Look for a common thread you can mention in one sentence to make the 
session cohesive. Keep it short: the Oral part has no spare time.
- Get to know the presenters. Check name pronunciations. Remind them to keep their mode of attendance up to date via the Google Form.
- T−4 days: tell the presenters their presentation position. If the paper is listed as 'Px-y Title...' on Miniconf and 
Slack, it is the y-th paper in Oral Session x.
- T−1 day: on your own laptop, test-open all slide submissions from the Sheet for Session Chair.

## On the day

Follow your runsheets (linked above) for the minute-by-minute steps. Suggestions for the hybrid parts:

### Oral part (first 30 min)

- When you introduce yourself, also introduce the [[Online Session Chair]] and say that online attendees can post questions 
on Slack in #oral-session-?. Welcome attendees to the session online. 
- Introduce each paper briefly, the same way for on-site and online presenters. For an online presenter, say they are 
joining online and the video is pre-recorded. 
- Keep time strictly: the Poster part and its Zoom Meeting start right after the Oral part.
- At the end, direct the audience to the posters, and mention that the online presenters are at the posters with the TVs.

### Poster part (60 min, with coffee)

- In the first 10 minutes, walk past every poster of your session and check that each presenter is there. Report 
issues to the [[Poster Session Helpdesk On-site]].
- On-site attendees tend to skip posters with an online presenter. Spend extra time there and bring people along.
- Ask on-site discussions to use the handheld mics, so online visitors in the breakout room can hear.
- If the [[Online Session Chair]] tells you that online visitors are waiting at a poster, let the on-site presenter know.
- At the end, thank the presenters.

The Tutorial is a hybrid session streamed over Zoom Webinar, with presenters who may be on-site or online. Presenters talk, and the audience asks questions at Q&A points chosen by the presenters.

### Your job
- Configure the Zoom Webinar under time constraints.
- Online presenters join the Webinar 10 minutes in advance: promote them to panelist (use [#panelist-names](https://ismir2026.slack.com/archives/C0CER5XPX19) to identify them) and test their audio+video input+output and screenshare.
- Online questions are text only, via the Zoom Webinar Q&A tool. Raising hands is not supported. The [[Tutorial Moderator]] tracks them.

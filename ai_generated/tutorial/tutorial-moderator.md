The Tutorial is a hybrid session streamed over Zoom Webinar, with presenters who may be on-site or online. Presenters talk, and the audience asks questions at Q&A points chosen by the presenters.

### Your job
- Serve as the in-room anchor on behalf of the remote tutorial chairs: introduce the tutorial and the presenters, keep the session on schedule, and coordinate with the room. The tutorial chairs train you separately.
- Greet the presenters, especially online ones.
- Be the on-site proxy for online presenters: speak up for them in the room, and tell them what the room sees and hears.
- Track questions: on-site questions first, then online questions from the Zoom Q&A panel.
- Questions do not interrupt presentations unless the presenters invite it.
- Online questions are text only, via the Zoom Webinar Q&A tool. Raising hands is not supported.

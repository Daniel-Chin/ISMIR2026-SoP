The Awards and Closing Session closes the conference: each chair or team presents the information that matters to everyone. It takes place on-site in the auditorium and is streamed over Zoom Webinar.

### Your job
- Configure the Zoom Webinar under time constraints.
- Online chairs who present join the Webinar 10 minutes in advance: promote them to panelist (use [#panelist-names](https://ismir2026.slack.com/archives/C0CER5XPX19) to identify them) and test their audio+video input+output and screenshare.
- There are Q&A sessions, but questions do not interrupt the presentations. Online questions are text only, via the Webinar Q&A tool; a [[Q&A Tracker]] on stage relays them.

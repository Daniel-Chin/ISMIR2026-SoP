The Awards and Closing Session closes the conference: each chair or team presents the information that matters to everyone. It takes place on-site in the auditorium and is streamed over Zoom Webinar.

### Your job
- View the Zoom Webinar remotely and report any issue on Slack.
- Several presenters take turns, and there are Q&A sessions: check that each speaker and each audience question is heard in the stream.

The Industry Panel is a hybrid session streamed over Zoom Webinar: 4 × 10-min presentations from the Platinum, Gold and Silver sponsors (1 presenter on-site, 3 online), then 30-min Q&A from the on-site and online audience. The on-site Moderator, Gus Xia, takes the transitions between speakers.

### Your job
- Join the Zoom Webinar remotely as a panelist.
- Announce the agenda at the start, and keep time: 10 min per presentation, 30 min for Q&A. The Moderator takes the transitions between speakers.
- Track the online Q&A: pick the most interesting online questions and read them to the panel.
- During Q&A, on-site questions come first, then online questions. No need to switch back and forth: wait for the Moderator to hand over to you.

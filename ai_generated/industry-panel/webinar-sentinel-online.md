The Industry Panel is a hybrid session streamed over Zoom Webinar: 3 × 10-min presentations from the Platinum, Gold and Silver sponsors (1 presenter on-site, 2 online), then a 30-min discussion and a 30-min Q&A from the on-site and online audience. The on-site Moderator, Gus Xia, announces the agenda, keeps time and takes the transitions between speakers.

### Your job
- View the Zoom Webinar remotely and report any issue on Slack.
- Several voices come from online (2 presenters and the [[Industry Chair]]): check that each is audible, that screen-shared slides and their sound come through, and that the on-site Moderator and presenter are heard too.

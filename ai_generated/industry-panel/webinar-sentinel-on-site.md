The Industry Panel is a hybrid session streamed over Zoom Webinar: 3 × 10-min presentations from the Platinum, Gold and Silver sponsors (1 presenter on-site, 2 online), then a 30-min discussion and a 30-min Q&A from the on-site and online audience. The on-site Moderator, Gus Xia, announces the agenda, keeps time and takes the transitions between speakers.

### Your job
- Sit on-site in the backstage but view only the Zoom Webinar on the provided laptop, with the provided headphones. Not only listen, but try to understand and enjoy the presentations --- you catch more issues this way.
- Keep the auditorium screen visible so any mismatch is caught, but pay main attention to the webinar.
- Pay special attention to the audio of the 2 online presenters (including shared sound) and the remote [[Industry Chair]], both in the webinar and in the room.
- If a panelist account is making noises, mute it.
- Best with sensitive hearing: you must distinguish headphone audio from room audio.

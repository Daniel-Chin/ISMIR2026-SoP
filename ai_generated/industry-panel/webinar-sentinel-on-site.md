The Industry Panel is a hybrid moderated discussion streamed over Zoom Webinar, with on-site Moderator Gus Xia and 4 sponsor panelists (1 on-site, 3 online), followed by Q&A from the on-site and online audience.

### Your job
- Sit on-site in the backstage but view only the Zoom Webinar on the provided laptop, with the provided headphones. Not only listen, but try to understand and enjoy the discussion --- you catch more issues this way.
- Keep the auditorium screen visible so any mismatch is caught, but pay main attention to the webinar.
- Pay special attention to the audio of the 3 online panelists and the remote [[Industry Chair]], both in the webinar and in the room.
- If a panelist account is making noises, mute it.
- Best with sensitive hearing: you must distinguish headphone audio from room audio.

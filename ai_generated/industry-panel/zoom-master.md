The Industry Panel is a hybrid session streamed over Zoom Webinar: 4 × 10-min presentations from the Platinum, Gold and Silver sponsors (1 presenter on-site, 3 online), then 30-min Q&A from the on-site and online audience. The on-site Moderator, Gus Xia, takes the transitions between speakers.

### Your job
- Configure the Zoom Webinar under time constraints.
- Promote to panelist: the 3 online presenters, the remote [[Industry Chair]], the Moderator, and the on-site presenter if presenting slides. Use [#panelist-names](https://ismir2026.slack.com/archives/C0CER5XPX19) to identify them.
- Test audio, video and screenshare with "Share sound" with each online presenter, and audio with the Industry Chair. Stay ready to fix their audio/video throughout.
- Between presentations, switch the screenshare to the next presenter.

### Q&A
- On-site questions first, then online questions. No need to switch back and forth.
- The remote Industry Chair picks the most interesting online questions and reads them to the panel. You clear the Q&A queue at the end.

The Industry Panel is a hybrid session streamed over Zoom Webinar: 3 × 10-min presentations from the Platinum, Gold and Silver sponsors (1 presenter on-site, 2 online), then a 30-min discussion and a 30-min Q&A from the on-site and online audience. The on-site Moderator, Gus Xia, announces the agenda, keeps time and takes the transitions between speakers.

### Your job
- Before the start, make sure the Moderator and the on-site presenter each have a mic.
- During the 30-min Q&A, distribute your mic to people asking questions in the audience.
- Sit down during the presentations and the discussion, but keep standing during Q&A.
- During Q&A, on-site questions are taken first, then online questions read by the remote [[Industry Chair]].
- Tip: don't take the mic back immediately after the question. Allow multi-round exchange.

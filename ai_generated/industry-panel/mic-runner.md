The Industry Panel is a hybrid moderated discussion streamed over Zoom Webinar, with on-site Moderator Gus Xia and 4 sponsor panelists (1 on-site, 3 online), followed by Q&A from the on-site and online audience.

### Your job
- Before the start, make sure the Moderator and the on-site panelist each have a mic.
- Distribute your mic to people asking questions in the audience.
- Sit down outside Q&A, but keep standing during Q&A.
- During Q&A, on-site questions are taken first, then online questions read by the remote [[Industry Chair]].
- Tip: don't take the mic back immediately after the question. Allow multi-round exchange.

- Runs 60 minutes.
- Hybrid: by each on-site poster is a TV that serves as a window to the online participants. One Zoom breakout room per paper. Not streamed on YouTube or Webinar.

### Your job
- You are assigned to one paper session, and attend its posters online.
- Before the conference, get to know the 10 presenters on Slack. During the session, engage with the online participants on Slack.
- If the presenter is on-site, online visitors see the poster and the author through the laptop webcam, and hear the on-site discussion through the mics.
- If the presenter is online, they join the breakout room and screenshare their poster.
- Online participants are directed to the Slack channel "Poster Session x", which has the link to the Poster Session Zoom room with a breakout room for each paper. To view a poster, they use its Miniconf paper page, entering from the Live Overview, the Miniconf paper list, or the Slack channel.
- Live Overview shows realtime online headcount at each poster: handy to find quiet rooms.
- Online, participants raise their hands or open their mic to ask.

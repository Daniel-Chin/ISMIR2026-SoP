The Poster Session runs 60 minutes in the Upper Atrium, A6.

- Hybrid: by each poster is a TV that serves as a window to the online participants. One Zoom breakout room per paper. Not streamed on YouTube or Webinar.

### Your job
- You are assigned to one paper session, and attend its posters on-site.
- Before the conference, get to know the 10 presenters. Confirm the session's presentation modality with them.
- If the presenter is on-site, online visitors join via the laptop webcam and the mics.
  - On-site discussions are encouraged to use the 2 handheld mics, so the online visitors can hear.
  - On-site presenters are trained to consciously divert attention to online participants.
- If the presenter is online, they appear on the TV and screenshare their poster. The handheld mic is free to take and pass around.
- Technical issues go to the [[Poster Session Helpdesk On-site]].

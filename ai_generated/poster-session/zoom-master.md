The Poster Session runs 60 minutes in the Upper Atrium, A6.

- Hybrid: by each poster is a TV that serves as a window to the online participants. One Zoom breakout room per paper. Not streamed on YouTube or Webinar.

### Your job
- Configure the Poster Session Zoom room and its breakout rooms under time constraints.
- Each paper has: a printed A0 poster + a laptop (on podium) + a TV + 2 cabled handheld directional mics + 1 simple audio interface.
- The laptop is in the paper's Zoom breakout room. Its audio input is the 2 handheld mics via the audio interface; its video input is the laptop webcam.
- If the presenter is on-site: on-site discussions are encouraged to use the mics for the online to hear. Face the webcam towards the poster and the author.
- If the presenter is online: they screenshare the poster on the TV. The handheld mic is free to take and pass around. Face the webcam towards the visitors.
- Online participants are directed to the Slack channel "Poster Session x", which has the link to the Poster Session Zoom room with a breakout room for each paper. To view a poster, they use its Miniconf paper page, entering from the Live Overview, the Miniconf paper list, or the Slack channel.
- Live Overview shows realtime online headcount at each poster.

### Troubleshoot cheatsheet
- Zoom's web-browser client has limited features. Instruct participants to download and install the [Zoom App](https://zoom.us/download).
- "Original sound for musicians" can be turned on to stop the noise filter from dropping sounds.
- Crosstalk between posters can be mitigated by lowering mic gain on the audio interface.

### Notes
- Known weak spots: power cables, mic cables. Crosstalk is mitigated by directional mics.
- Bandwidth = 0.6*10 = 6 mbps. 2.4GHz Wifi offers 60mbps, which is more than enough bandwidth.

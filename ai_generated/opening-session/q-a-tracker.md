The opening welcome of the conference: each chair or team presents the information that matters to everyone. On-site in the auditorium, streamed over Zoom Webinar.

### Your job
- Sit on stage, not introduced. Relay online questions to the presenters.
- Online questions arrive as text in the Zoom Webinar Q&A tool.
- There are Q&A sessions, but questions do not interrupt the presentations. During Q&A, on-site questions are taken first, then the online ones you relay. No switching back and forth.
- Your mic goes into the stream, so online attendees hear the questions you read.

The opening welcome of the conference: each chair or team presents the information that matters to everyone. On-site in the auditorium, streamed over Zoom Webinar.

### Your job
- Distribute your mic to people asking questions in the audience, so they are heard online too.
- There are Q&A sessions, but questions do not interrupt the presentations. Sit down outside Q&A; keep standing during Q&A.
- On-site questions are taken first, then online ones.
- Tip: don't take the mic back immediately after the question. Allow multi-round exchange.

The Opening Session is the conference's opening welcome: each chair or team presents the information that matters to everyone. It takes place on-site in the auditorium and is streamed over Zoom Webinar.

### Your job
- You are the wizard of the room system.
- Several presenters take turns on stage, and there are Q&A sessions with audience mics.
- Into the Webinar sound input go: the speaker mics, the stage mic and the audience mics.

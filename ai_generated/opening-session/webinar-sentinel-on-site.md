The Opening Session is the conference's opening welcome: each chair or team presents the information that matters to everyone. It takes place on-site in the auditorium and is streamed over Zoom Webinar.

### Your job
- Sit on-site in the backstage, but view only the Zoom Webinar on the provided laptop, with the provided headphones.
- Not only listen, but try to understand and enjoy the presentations: you catch more issues this way.
- Keep the auditorium screen visible so any mismatch is caught, but pay main attention to the webinar.
- Several presenters take turns, and there are Q&A sessions: check that each speaker and each audience mic is heard in the stream.
- If a panelist account is making noises, mute it.

The Oral Session runs 30 minutes in the 300-capacity auditorium A6-008 and is streamed over Zoom Webinar. It holds 10 presentations of 3 minutes each, back to back, with no Q&A.

- If the presenter is on-site, they present on stage. Otherwise, the pre-recorded video already on Miniconf is played.

### Your job
- You are assigned to one paper session, and attend it on-site.
- Before the conference, get to know the 10 presenters. Confirm the session's presentation schedule and modality with them.
- Arrange timing and seating: on-site presenters sit in the front row. Presentations use the stage laptop by default.
- Introduce every talk. Check name pronunciations beforehand. Be brief, nice, and non-distracting.
- Keep time during the presentations.
- Your mic and the presenter mic go into the stream.

### Google Sheet "For Session Chair"
- [Sheet for Session Chair](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JPkY1TY8BfC07ZicIe6FDiZt94PALu3r6AFXhavpwlY/edit?gid=1217880391#gid=1217880391)
- Grouped by session, sorted by presentation order. Each paper shows:
  - CMT ID, title, session_index, position_in_session, chair_onsite, chair_remote, pre-recorded_video, slides_pdf, slides_preferred.

### Stage laptops
- Two stage laptops, preferably MacBook (for maximum compatibility).
  - Keep open the [Sheet for Session Chair](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JPkY1TY8BfC07ZicIe6FDiZt94PALu3r6AFXhavpwlY/edit?gid=1217880391#gid=1217880391).
  - Keep open the local folder containing all downloaded files.
  - One window at a time. Open slides, close slides. Open video, close video.

### Slides
- The on-site presenters submit their slides directly to the Virtual Team, who prepares the Sheet and reminds authors about overdue slides. Deadline: 2 days before the conference.
- A PDF is always there as a failsafe; a presenter may also have a Powerpoint file, a Keynote file, or a Google Slides link.
- After the deadline, when authors request to update slides, the tech team handles the update. Session chairs do not edit the Sheet.

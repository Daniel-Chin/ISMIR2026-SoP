The Oral Session runs 30 minutes in the 300-capacity auditorium A6-008 and is streamed over Zoom Webinar. It holds 10 presentations of 3 minutes each, back to back, with no Q&A.

- If the presenter is on-site, they present on stage. Otherwise, the pre-recorded video already on Miniconf is played.
- The [[On-site Session Chair]] introduces every talk.

### Your job
- Sit on-site in the backstage, but view only the Zoom Webinar on the provided laptop, with the provided headphones.
- Not only listen, but try to understand and enjoy the talks: you catch more issues this way.
- Keep the auditorium screen visible so any mismatch is caught, but pay main attention to the webinar.
- If a panelist account is making noises, mute it.

### What the stream should carry
- The [[On-site Session Chair]] mic & presenter mic route through the PA system into the Zoom sound input. Laptop audio, including the pre-recorded videos, goes through the Auditorium system into the Webinar.
- Talks switch every 3 minutes, alternating between live presenters and videos: check each switch.

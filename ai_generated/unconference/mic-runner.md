The Unconference is a hybrid session with no predefined program, run on a Zoom Meeting: a short plenary in A6-008 introduces the topics participants proposed on [Dotstorming](https://dotstorming.com/b/6aea16167c45b114dc388cf8), then participants split into groups across 4 hybrid rooms (A6-008, A6-007, A6-005, A6-004) for ~45 minutes of informal discussion.

### Your job
- Each room has one Mic Runner. During the plenary in A6-008, bring the mic to on-site participants who speak; then go to your on-site room for the discussion.
- Every on-site voice must go through a mic: the on-site room joins its Zoom breakout room through the room computer, and online participants only hear what reaches the mic.
- Sit down when no one is speaking, but keep standing while participants speak up.
- Tip: don't take the mic back immediately after someone speaks. Allow multi-round exchange.

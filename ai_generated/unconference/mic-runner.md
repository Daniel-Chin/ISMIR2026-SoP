The Unconference is a hybrid session with no predefined program, run on a Zoom Meeting: a short plenary introduces the topics participants proposed, then participants split into groups for ~45 minutes of informal discussion.

### Your job
- During the opening plenary, distribute your mic to on-site participants who speak.
- Sit down when no one is speaking, but keep standing while participants are speaking up.
- Tip: don't take the mic back immediately after someone speaks. Allow multi-round exchange.

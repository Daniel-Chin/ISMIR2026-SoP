The Unconference is a session with no predefined program, run on a Zoom Meeting: a hybrid opening plenary in A6-008 picks topics participants proposed on [Dotstorming](https://dotstorming.com/b/6aea16167c45b114dc388cf8), then 2 on-site groups (A6-008, A6-007) and 2 online groups (Zoom breakout rooms) discuss separately for ~45 minutes before reconvening, hybrid, in A6-008.

### Your job
- During the plenary and the reconvene in A6-008, which are hybrid, bring the mic to on-site participants who speak, so online participants hear them.
- During the discussion, go to your assigned on-site room.
- Sit down when no one is speaking, but keep standing while participants speak up.
- Tip: don't take the mic back immediately after someone speaks. Allow multi-round exchange.

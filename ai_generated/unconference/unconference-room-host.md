The Unconference is a session with no predefined program, run on a Zoom Meeting: a hybrid opening plenary in A6-008 picks topics participants proposed on [Dotstorming](https://dotstorming.com/b/6aea16167c45b114dc388cf8), then 2 on-site groups (A6-008, A6-007) and 2 online groups (Zoom breakout rooms) discuss separately for ~45 minutes before reconvening, hybrid, in A6-008.

### Your job
- Host one Unconference group, either on-site (A6-008 or A6-007) or online (a Zoom breakout room). The [[Unconference Chair]] assigns you a topic and announces you as its host at the end of the plenary.
- Facilitate ~45 minutes of discussion, and have the group pick a presenter to share its conclusions at the reconvene.
- If you host in A6-007, bring the group back to A6-008 for the reconvene.

### Your group
- Discussions are not hybrid: on-site rooms are not connected to Zoom, and online groups meet only in their breakout room.
- Online hosts are promoted to Zoom co-host in the hub during the plenary, so they can move between breakout rooms.
- Formats: discussions, short informal presentations, demos, brainstorming, etc. No preparation required.

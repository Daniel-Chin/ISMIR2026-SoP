The Unconference is a session run on a Zoom Meeting, not a Webinar, because it uses breakout rooms: a hybrid plenary in A6-008 on the Meeting hub, then ~45 minutes of discussion in 4 Zoom breakout rooms and 2 on-site rooms, then a hybrid reconvene in A6-008.

### Your job
- Configure the Zoom Meeting and its breakout rooms under time constraints.
- Keep breakout rooms closed until the online vote is done. Then create 4 breakout rooms named after the top 4 topics, without waiting for the on-site vote.
- Anyone can join any breakout room: online participants choose their own, guided by a notice you share in the hub.
- On-site rooms are not connected to Zoom during the discussion. A6-008 reconnects to the hub for the reconvene, after the breakout rooms close.

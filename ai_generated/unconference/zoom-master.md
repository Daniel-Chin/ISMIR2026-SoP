The Unconference is a hybrid session run on a Zoom Meeting, not a Webinar, because it uses breakout rooms: a short plenary in the main room, then ~45 minutes of discussion across 4 hybrid rooms.

### Your job
- Configure the Zoom Meeting and its breakout rooms under time constraints.
- Each of the 4 on-site rooms is paired with one Zoom breakout room. The on-site room joins its breakout room as one Zoom account (room computer + camera + mic).
- Make the 4 room hosts (the [[Unconference Chair]] and the [[Unconference Room Host]]s) co-hosts so they can move between rooms.
- Online participants choose their own breakout room.

The Unconference is a hybrid session run on a Zoom Meeting: a short plenary in A6-008, then ~45 minutes of discussion across 4 hybrid rooms (A6-008, A6-007, A6-005, A6-004).

### Your job
- You are the wizard of the room systems of A6-007 and A6-008. (The [[Zoom Master]] sets up A6-004 and A6-005.)

### Rooms
- The plenary in A6-008 is streamed on the Zoom Meeting.
- During the breakout, each on-site room joins its Zoom breakout room as one Zoom account (room computer + camera + mic).
- Each room has a [[Mic Runner]]; every on-site voice must go through a mic so online participants can hear.

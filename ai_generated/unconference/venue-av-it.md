The Unconference is a session run on a Zoom Meeting: a hybrid plenary in A6-008, then ~45 minutes of non-hybrid discussion in on-site rooms and Zoom breakout rooms, then a hybrid reconvene in A6-008.

### Your job
- You are the wizard of A6-008's room system.
- The plenary in A6-008 is connected to the Zoom Meeting hub.
- At the end of the plenary, disconnect A6-008 from Zoom: an on-site group discusses there without Zoom.
- At the reconvene, reconnect A6-008 to the Zoom hub.

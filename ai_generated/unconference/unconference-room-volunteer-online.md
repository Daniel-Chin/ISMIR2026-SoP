The Unconference is a session with no predefined program, run on a Zoom Meeting: a hybrid opening plenary in A6-008 picks the top 4 topics participants proposed on [Dotstorming](https://dotstorming.com/b/6aea16167c45b114dc388cf8); all 4 are discussed online in Zoom breakout rooms, and 2 of them on-site (A6-008, A6-007), for ~45 minutes before reconvening, hybrid, in A6-008.

### Your job
- Support one of the 4 Zoom breakout rooms, which open right after the online vote.
- Make sure people can join your breakout room. Anyone can join any room; online participants choose their own room, guided by a notice in the hub.
- At the start of the discussion, introduce yourself as the volunteer and announce that the group needs a Room Host. Make sure someone takes the [[Unconference Room Host]] role. The host need not be pre-assigned and can emerge during the discussion; they moderate, keep notes, and present the group's conclusions at the reconvene.
- Keep time: the discussion runs ~45 minutes.

### Your room
- Discussions are not hybrid: online groups meet only in their breakout room.
- Formats: discussions, short informal presentations, demos, brainstorming, etc. No preparation required.

The Unconference is a session with no predefined program, run on a Zoom Meeting: a hybrid opening plenary in A6-008 picks the top 4 topics participants proposed on [Dotstorming](https://dotstorming.com/b/6aea16167c45b114dc388cf8); all 4 are discussed online in Zoom breakout rooms, and 2 of them on-site (A6-008, A6-007), for ~45 minutes before reconvening, hybrid, in A6-008.

### Your job
- Run the opening plenary in A6-008, on the Zoom Meeting hub: participants vote on topics online using Dotstorming, and the top 4 topics are taken. Online breakout rooms open right after, for all 4 topics.
- Run an on-site vote among the top 4 to select 2 on-site topics. Announce each with its room: A6-008 or A6-007.
- Do not take a breakout room role.
- At the reconvene, invite each group's [[Unconference Room Host]] to share its conclusions: 4 online and 2 on-site. Then close the session for everyone, on-site and online.

### Format
- Topics: anything MIR-relevant (technical challenges, research directions, community initiatives, social/ethical issues, ...).
- Formats: discussions, short informal presentations, demos, brainstorming, etc. No preparation required.
- Discussions are not hybrid: A6-008 disconnects from Zoom during the discussion and reconnects for the reconvene.
- Each group has a pre-assigned volunteer, who makes sure people can join, makes sure someone takes the Room Host role, and keeps time. The Room Host can emerge during the discussion; they moderate, keep notes, and present conclusions.

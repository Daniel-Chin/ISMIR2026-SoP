The Unconference is a session with no predefined program, run on a Zoom Meeting: a hybrid opening plenary in A6-008 picks topics participants proposed on [Dotstorming](https://dotstorming.com/b/6aea16167c45b114dc388cf8), then 2 on-site groups (A6-008, A6-007) and 2 online groups (Zoom breakout rooms) discuss separately for ~45 minutes before reconvening, hybrid, in A6-008.

### Your job
- Run the opening plenary in A6-008, on the Zoom Meeting hub: participants vote on topics online using Dotstorming, and the top 4 topics are taken.
- Run an on-site vote among the top 4 to select the 2 on-site topics. Announce each topic with its room and host: the 2 on-site topics go to A6-008 and A6-007, and the remaining 2 run online as Zoom breakout rooms, each with an online host you announce.
- Host one of the groups as an [[Unconference Room Host]] during the discussion.
- At the reconvene, invite each group's presenter to share its conclusions: 2 online conclusions and 2 on-site conclusions. Then close the session for everyone, on-site and online.

### Format
- Topics: anything MIR-relevant (technical challenges, research directions, community initiatives, social/ethical issues, ...).
- Formats: discussions, short informal presentations, demos, brainstorming, etc. No preparation required.
- Discussions are not hybrid: A6-008 disconnects from Zoom during the discussion and reconnects for the reconvene.
- Each group picks a presenter for the reconvene.

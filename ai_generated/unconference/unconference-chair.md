The Unconference is a hybrid session with no predefined program, run on a Zoom Meeting: a short plenary introduces the topics participants proposed on [Dotstorming](https://dotstorming.com/b/6aea16167c45b114dc388cf8), then participants split into groups across 4 hybrid rooms for ~45 minutes of informal discussion.

### Your job
- Run the opening plenary in the main room: introduce the topics proposed on Dotstorming, identify those attracting the most interest, and form the groups, one per room.
- Then host one of the 4 rooms as an [[Unconference Room Host]]: facilitate the discussion and balance on-site and online participation.
- At the end, make a concluding statement to everyone, on-site and online.

### Format
- Topics: anything MIR-relevant (technical challenges, research directions, community initiatives, social/ethical issues, ...).
- Formats: discussions, short informal presentations, demos, brainstorming, etc. No preparation required.
- Each on-site room is paired with one Zoom breakout room; the on-site room joins its breakout room as one Zoom account (room computer + camera + mic).

The WIMIR Session is a hybrid talk + Q&A session streamed over Zoom Webinar, in which all speakers are online and screenshare their slides from their own computers.

### Your job
- Configure the Zoom Webinar under time constraints.
- Promote to panelist: all online speakers and the [[Q&A Tracker]]. Use [#panelist-names](https://ismir2026.slack.com/archives/C0CER5XPX19) to identify them.
- Test audio, video, screenshare and sound share with each online speaker, then stay ready to fix them and to switch between speakers throughout.

### Q&A
- On-site questions first, then online questions (text only, via the Zoom Webinar Q&A tool), relayed by the Q&A Tracker. No need to switch back and forth.

The WIMIR Session is a hybrid talk + Q&A session streamed over Zoom Webinar, in which all speakers are online and screenshare their slides from their own computers.

### Your job
- Sit on-site in the backstage but view only the Zoom Webinar on the provided laptop, with the provided headphones. Not only listen, but try to understand and enjoy the talks --- you catch more issues this way.
- Keep the auditorium screen visible so any mismatch is caught, but pay main attention to the webinar.
- Pay special attention to the online speakers' audio (voice and slides sound share), both in the webinar and in the room.
- If a panelist account is making noises, mute it.

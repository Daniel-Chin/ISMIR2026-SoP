The WIMIR Session is a hybrid talk + Q&A session streamed over Zoom Webinar, in which all speakers are online and screenshare their slides from their own computers.

### Your job
- Distribute your mic to people asking questions in the audience. Your mic is mixed into the Zoom Webinar sound input, so this is how the online Speaker and online attendees hear them.
- Sit down outside Q&A, but keep standing during Q&A.
- During Q&A, on-site questions are taken first, then online questions.
- Tip: don't take the mic back immediately after the question. Allow multi-round exchange.

The WIMIR Session is a hybrid talk + Q&A session streamed over Zoom Webinar, in which all speakers are online and screenshare their slides from their own computers.

### Your job
- Sit on stage, not introduced. Relay online questions to the Speaker, and mark each as answered.
- Online questions are text only, via the Zoom Webinar Q&A tool. Raising hands is not supported.
- During Q&A, on-site questions come first, then online questions. No need to switch back and forth.
- The Speaker is remote, so you may need to barge in: "We have a question. Is now a good time?".
- Your mic is mixed into the Zoom Webinar sound input, so online attendees hear the questions you read.

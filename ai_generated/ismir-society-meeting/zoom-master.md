The ISMIR Society Meeting is a board meeting in which the conference chairs meet the ISMIR steering committee, run as a hybrid panel discussion with some panelists on-site and some online, and broadcast to all conference attendees over Zoom Webinar.

### Your job
- Configure the Zoom Webinar under time constraints.
- Promote to panelist: all online panelists, on-site panelists presenting slides, and the [[Q&A Tracker]]. Use [#panelist-names](https://ismir2026.slack.com/archives/C0CER5XPX19) to identify them.
- Test audio and video with each online panelist, then stay ready to fix their audio/video throughout.
- Remind [[Venue AV/IT]] to display the online panelists on the stage-facing TV.

### Q&A
- On-site questions first, then online questions. No need to switch back and forth.
- The [[Q&A Tracker]] relays online questions to the panel.

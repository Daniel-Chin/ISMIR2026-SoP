The ISMIR Society Meeting is a board meeting in which the conference chairs meet the ISMIR steering committee, run as a hybrid panel discussion with some panelists on-site and some online, and broadcast to all conference attendees over Zoom Webinar.

### Your job
- Before the start, make sure every on-site panelist has a mic.
- Distribute your mic to people asking questions in the audience.
- Sit down outside Q&A, but keep standing during Q&A.
- During Q&A, on-site questions are taken first, then online questions relayed by the [[Q&A Tracker]].
- Tip: don't take the mic back immediately after the question. Allow multi-round exchange.

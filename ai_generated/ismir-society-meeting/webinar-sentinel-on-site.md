The ISMIR Society Meeting is a board meeting in which the conference chairs meet the ISMIR steering committee, run as a hybrid panel discussion with some panelists on-site and some online, and broadcast to all conference attendees over Zoom Webinar.

### Your job
- Sit on-site in the backstage but view only the Zoom Webinar on the provided laptop, with the provided headphones. Not only listen, but try to understand and enjoy the discussion --- you catch more issues this way.
- Keep the auditorium screen visible so any mismatch is caught, but pay main attention to the webinar.
- Pay special attention to the online panelists' audio, both in the webinar and in the room.
- If a panelist account is making noises, mute it.

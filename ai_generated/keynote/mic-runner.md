The Keynote is a 60-minute talk by an invited Speaker, who may be on-site or remote, followed by Q&A. It takes place in the 300-capacity auditorium A6-008 and is streamed via Zoom Webinar 500.

### Your job
- Distribute your mic to people asking questions in the audience. Your mic is mixed into the Zoom Webinar sound input, so this is how online attendees hear them.
- Sit down outside Q&A, but keep standing during Q&A.
- The Speaker may also take questions during the talk, so be ready throughout.
- During the Q&A, on-site questions are taken first, then online questions.
- Tip: don't take the mic back immediately after the question. Allow multi-round exchange.

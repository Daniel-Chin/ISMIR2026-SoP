A 60-minute talk by an invited Speaker, who may be on-site or remote, followed by Q&A. Onsite location: 300-capacity auditorium A6-008. Streamed via Zoom Webinar 500.

### Your job
- Configure the Zoom Webinar under time constraints.

### Q&A
- Online questions are text only, via the Zoom Webinar Q&A tool. Raising hands is not supported.
- A [[Q&A Producer]] on stage organizes them and relays them to the Speaker.

### Stage
- Two stage laptops, preferably MacBook (for maximum compatibility).
- The Speaker may use any laptop for slides. That laptop is connected to the Auditorium HDMI.

### Sound
- Audio mix that goes into the Zoom Webinar sound input:
  - Speaker mic
  - [[Keynote Inviter]] mic
  - [[Q&A Producer]] mic
  - [[Mic Runner]]s mic
- On-site slides audio goes through the Auditorium system into the Webinar. Online slides audio goes via screenshare.

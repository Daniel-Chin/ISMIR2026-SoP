A 60-minute talk by an invited Speaker, who may be on-site or remote, followed by Q&A. Onsite location: 300-capacity auditorium A6-008. Streamed via Zoom Webinar 500.

### Your job
- Sit on stage, not introduced. Clarify and relay online questions to the Speaker.
- Online questions are text only, via the Zoom Webinar Q&A tool. Raising hands is not supported.
- Organize and clarify the text questions before relaying them.
- We support not only a Q&A session but also live speaker-audience interaction. Use cards / gestures for robust & confident flow: when there is a clarified question, no matter during the talk or during Q&A, signal (e.g. raise a hand). When the Speaker takes the question, read it.
  - If the Speaker is remote, you have to barge in some of the times: "We have a question. Is now a good time?".
- During the Q&A, on-site questions come first, then online questions. No switching back and forth.
- Your mic is mixed into the Zoom Webinar sound input, so online attendees hear the questions you read.

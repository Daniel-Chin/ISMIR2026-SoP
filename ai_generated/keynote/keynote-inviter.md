A 60-minute talk by an invited Speaker, who may be on-site or remote, followed by Q&A. Onsite location: 300-capacity auditorium A6-008. Streamed via Zoom Webinar 500.

### Your job
- Introduce the Speaker.
- Keep the time during the presentation and the Q&A.
- Optionally serve as another [[Q&A Producer]].
- Your mic is mixed into the Webinar, so online attendees hear your introduction and time cues.
- The livestream shows only the podium area, not the entire stage: stay in the camera view.

### Q&A
- On-site & online questions are supported. Besides the Q&A session, the Speaker may take questions during the talk.
- Online questions are text only. A [[Q&A Producer]] sits on stage with you, organizes and clarifies them, signals (e.g. raises a hand) when one is ready, and reads it when the Speaker takes it.
- During the Q&A, take on-site questions first, then online questions. No switching back and forth.
- A [[Mic Runner]] gives their mic to each on-site person asking.

### Stage
- The Speaker may use any laptop for slides, connected to the Auditorium HDMI.

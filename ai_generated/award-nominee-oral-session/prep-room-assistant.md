Oral presentations of the award-nominated papers. Each paper gets a 10-min presentation and a 5-min Q&A. Onsite location: 300-capacity auditorium A6-008. Streamed via Zoom Webinar 500.
- Some presenters are on-site; some are remote and present live via Zoom Webinar; some cannot attend live and share a 10 minute video that is played instead.

### Your job
- Run the prep room: a separate Zoom Meeting for the online presenters. You will miss the session itself.
- An online presenter joins the prep room 10 minutes before the scheduled presentation.
- Help them figure out audio+video input+output, as well as screenshare with sound. They manage their own slides and present them via screenshare.
- When it's time, let the presenter quit the prep room and join the main Webinar, where the [[Zoom Master]] promotes them to panelist.

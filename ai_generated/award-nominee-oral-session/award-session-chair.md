The Award Nominee Oral Session presents the award-nominated papers: each paper gets a 10-minute presentation and a 5-minute Q&A. It takes place in the 300-capacity auditorium A6-008 and is streamed via Zoom Webinar 500.

### Your job
- Host the session, on-site.
- Before the conference, get to know the presenters. Confirm the session's presentation schedule and modality with them:
  - Some presenters are on-site; some are remote and present live via Zoom Webinar.
  - If they cannot attend live, they share a 10 minute video, which you play instead of a live presenter.
- Arrange timing and seating within the session.
- Introduce every talk. Check name pronunciations beforehand.
- Keep time during the presentations.
- Start and end each Q&A.

### Online presenters
- They join a separate Zoom Meeting, the prep room, 10 minutes before their presentation. A [[Prep Room Assistant]] checks their audio, video and screenshare with sound there.
- When it's time, the presenter quits the prep room and joins the main Webinar, where the [[Zoom Master]] promotes them to panelist.
- They manage their own slides.

### Q&A
- Questions do not interrupt the presentation.
- On-site & online questions are supported. Take on-site questions first, then online questions. No switching back and forth.
- When you take an on-site question, a [[Mic Runner]] gives their mic to the person asking.
- Online questions are text only, via the Zoom Webinar Q&A tool. A [[Q&A Tracker]] organizes them and relays them when you turn to them.

### Stage laptops
- Two stage laptops, preferably MacBook (for maximum compatibility). The presenter uses the stage laptop for slides.
  - Keep open the [Sheet for Award Session Chair](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JPkY1TY8BfC07ZicIe6FDiZt94PALu3r6AFXhavpwlY/edit?gid=332004949#gid=332004949).
  - Keep open the local folder containing all downloaded slides.
  - One window at a time. Open slides, close slides.

### Slides
- These 10-minute slides are distinct from the 3-min slides for the [Oral Session](../oral-session/).
- The on-site presenters submit their slides directly to the Virtual Team, who prepares the Sheet and reminds authors about overdue slides. Deadline: 2 days before the conference.
- A PDF is always there as a failsafe; a presenter may also have a Powerpoint file, a Keynote file, or a Google Slides link.
- After the deadline, when authors request to update slides, the tech team handles the update. Session chairs do not edit the Sheet.

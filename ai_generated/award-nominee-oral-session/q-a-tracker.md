The Award Nominee Oral Session presents the award-nominated papers: each paper gets a 10-minute presentation and a 5-minute Q&A. It takes place in the 300-capacity auditorium A6-008 and is streamed via Zoom Webinar 500.
- Some presenters are on-site; some are remote and present live via Zoom Webinar; some cannot attend live and share a 10 minute video that is played instead.

### Your job
- Sit on stage, not introduced, with the [[Award Session Chair]] at your laptop in the corner of the stage. Relay online questions to the presenter.
- Your laptop is logged into the Webinar as a panelist; the [[Award Session Chair]] also uses it to send time cues to online presenters via Zoom Chat.
- Online questions are text only, via the Zoom Webinar Q&A tool. Raising hands is not supported.
- Organize the text questions before relaying them. Questions do not interrupt the presentation: text questions arriving during the presentation wait for the Q&A.
- The [[Award Session Chair]] starts and ends each Q&A, and takes on-site questions first, then turns to you for online questions. No need to switch back and forth.
- Your mic is mixed into the Zoom Webinar sound input, so online attendees hear the questions you read.

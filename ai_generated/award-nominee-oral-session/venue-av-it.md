The Award Nominee Oral Session presents the award-nominated papers: each paper gets a 10-minute presentation and a 5-minute Q&A. It takes place in the 300-capacity auditorium A6-008 and is streamed via Zoom Webinar 500.
- Some presenters are on-site; some are remote and present live via Zoom Webinar; some cannot attend live and share a 10 minute video that is played instead.

### Your job
- You are the wizard of the room system.

### Stage
- Two stage laptops, preferably MacBook (for maximum compatibility).
- The presenter uses the stage laptop for slides. That laptop is connected to the Auditorium HDMI.

### Sound
- Audio mix that goes into the Zoom Webinar sound input:
  - Presenter mic
  - [[Award Session Chair]] mic
  - [[Q&A Tracker]] mic
  - [[Mic Runner]]s mic
- On-site slides audio goes through the Auditorium system into the Webinar. Online slides audio goes via screenshare.
- Online video playback is to be supported as backup.
